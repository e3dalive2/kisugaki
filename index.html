FAQ 🌸
What software do you use for facial tracking? Do I need an iPhone?
For face tracking I use iFacialMocap or VTube Studio (both on iPhone) through VSeeFace on PC. So yes, you need an iPhone. For Android there's Meowface, though quality differs significantly from iOS tracking.
Why do you use VSeeFace? In what format will I receive the model?
I prefer VSeeFace because of its VSFAvatar format, which enables many features unavailable in VRM. If you use another app, I can prepare a VRM model instead.
What files will I receive besides the model?
You'll receive .FBX, Blender (.BLEND), Unity project, and .TGA/.PNG textures. .PSD files are available on request.
Can you port my model to VRChat?
I can port models I've created to VRChat. I don't do customization for models not made by me, and may decline depending on workload. I do not prepare .PMX format for MMD.
Do you accept characters of any style?
No — I only work with anime-style characters. I don't accept furry, robots, or male characters (unless accessory to the main humanoid). Acceptance is also based on personal preference.
What should the reference art look like?
You need concept art with front, back, and side views. No pencil sketches or low-detail references. Additional images showing textures or expressions are welcome. You must have the rights to use the art.
Can I change the design after work has started?
Minor mesh tweaks may be possible. Significant design changes after work begins are not allowed. Please be 100% sure before submitting your commission.
How does payment work?
50/50 split — I begin after the first 50%, and deliver after the remaining 50%. Under $500 requires full payment upfront. Wise preferred; PayPal also accepted (+~$20 per transfer).
